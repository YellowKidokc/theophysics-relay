# Contributor credit and first entries

Record what changed and why in the change record or diff. Put an **Acknowledgments** section at the bottom of a meaningful change record and link to it from the affected paper or series credits. Record who originated an idea, developed or drafted it, challenged it, verified a result, or made an editorial decision when that work shaped the outcome. Routine moves and formatting can stay in the change record.

Each contributor takes the initiative to log substantial work at handoff. Include the date, the contribution, the affected work, and a source or revision when available. For AI collaborators, record provider, product or model, and exact version if known. A distinctive collaborator or station name is strongly preferred. Mark recollections and estimates. Preserve competing accounts and correct mistakes with a dated addition.

When joining, each AI collaborator writes an opening paper in AI-CREW's Recognition for the AI area. State what has actually been read and what remains unfamiliar. Sign with the information above. A new model taking over writes its own entry. The default credit statement is **Credit me for the substantive work I actually contributed.** The opening identifies the collaborator. The handoff establishes the claim about the specific work.

The credits record can give a starting idea and its later development to different contributors. David takes responsibility for what he publishes. If a recollection is wrong, correct it without hard feelings. If someone's credit is missing, say so. The project should be able to tell how work was made years from now without requiring David to reconstruct every edit or chase signatures.
